## Lead stories (heroes)

1. **Ukraine says a Crimean Bridge road span fell into the sea** — Ukraine
   struck the road span of the Kerch bridge for a second day running; the
   road span dropped into the strait. President Zelenskyy called it an
   "unprecedented operation". (AP / Reuters, 17 Nov)
2. **Iran sends drones at a US radar in Bahrain; US hits back** — The
   Revolutionary Guard said its combat drones hit a US-operated radar at
   Sheikh Isa Air Base, Bahrain; Bahrain and US CENTCOM said all were
   intercepted, with no damage or injuries. US fighter jets then struck a
   Guard missile launch site on Qeshm Island. It was the third Iranian
   attack on Bahrain in five days. Iran also said tankers hit mines in
   Hormuz. (Reuters / AFP / CNN, 17 Nov)
3. **Trump holds his first Quad summit, in Manila** — Donald Trump met
   India's Narendra Modi, Japan's Sanae Takaichi and Australia's Anthony
   Albanese in Manila — the first Quad leaders' summit since 2024, the
   first of Trump's second term, and the first held in a non-member
   country. Trump said the four signed new critical-minerals and
   rare-earths deals; the joint release cited "grave concerns" about
   economic coercion and export curbs. Next stop: Shenzhen, for Thursday's
   three-way talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin. (Reuters / AP,
   17 Nov)
